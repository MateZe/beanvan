recorded by 15HPanska_Ruttner_Jan and released under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Both files were trimmed, faded, normalized, and converted to 44.1 kHz mono PCM
for Beanvan. No attribution is required.
